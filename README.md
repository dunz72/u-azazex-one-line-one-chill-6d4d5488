One line of horror — a single sentence under 200 characters:

> My daughter's voice came through the baby monitor again last night, whispering my name from the room she never slept in — the one we bricked up in 2019.

`main.py` is a stdlib-only copier: it reads `entry.txt` next to it and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validating that the line is non-empty, under 200 characters, and a single line, then verifying the output with SHA-256.
